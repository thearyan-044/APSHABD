THE ORIGIN

BORN AT
MIDNIGHT.
MADE IN
CHENNAI.

Pin Drop Silence started as a late-night idea that refused to sleep. One safety pin, one drop, one city — and a belief that the loudest statement is the one you wear, not the one you shout.

Every piece carries a neighborhood on it. Not Brooklyn. Not NY. Your area — in your script, on your chest.

We're stitching modern western streetwear to Indian streets: varsity cuts with Tamil type, heavyweight tees with Devanagari, drops named after the roads you grew up on.
