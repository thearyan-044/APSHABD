01 / The idea Made in India

Not merch.
Memory you
can wear.

The label began with one question: why should Indian streetwear borrow someone else's street?

So we started closer to home. With the station you missed, the road you grew up on, the corner that still knows your name. Every piece is a record of a neighbourhood — set in its own script, carried in its own rhythm.

Western silhouettes. Indian coordinates. No costume. No souvenir. Just the places that made you, cut into something worth keeping.
