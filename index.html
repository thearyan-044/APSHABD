THE ORIGIN

BORN AT
MIDNIGHT.
MADE FOR
INDIA.

PD Silence started as a late-night idea that refused to sleep. One 10 year old memory, one idea on the quietest man/woman in the room, and a belief that the loudest statement is the one you wear, not the one you shout.

Every piece carries a neighborhood on it. Not Brooklyn. Not NY. Your area — in your script, on your back.

We got to using this piece of clothing as a canvas to portray a statement, not an art. By stitching modern western streetwear to Indian streets varsity cuts with the desi look, heavyweight tees with the concert vibes, drops named after the roads you grew up on. From your first slur, to your last bitch ass ex. From the first long walk you took, to the place you go to earn a living, we tryna cover it all. So drop in any new locations you got in mind, we shall drop in to resonate for you.
